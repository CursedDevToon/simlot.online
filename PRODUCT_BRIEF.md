# MASTER BUILD PROMPT — SIMLOT.COM
## The World's Free AI-Powered Opportunity Discovery Platform

Build a complete, modern, responsive, production-ready website called **SIMLOT**, accessible through **Simlot.com**.

### 1. THE VISION

SIMLOT is a completely free global platform that helps students and young people discover opportunities to develop their skills, gain experience, earn recognition, strengthen their CVs and build competitive applications for universities, scholarships and future careers.

**Main tagline:** "Your Potential. Unlimited Opportunities."

**Secondary tagline:** "Discover opportunities. Build your future."

The platform must help users find:

- Academic competitions and Olympiads
- International competitions and challenges
- Scholarships and bursaries
- Grants and funding opportunities
- Essay and writing competitions
- STEM, mathematics and science competitions
- Coding, robotics and hackathons
- Research opportunities
- Leadership programmes
- Volunteering opportunities
- Internships and work experience
- Summer schools and enrichment programmes
- Entrepreneurship and innovation challenges
- Creative arts, music and design competitions
- Public speaking and debating competitions
- Environmental and sustainability initiatives
- University preparation programmes
- Awards, fellowships and youth recognition programmes

The platform must be useful to students worldwide, not just students in the UK or USA.

**SIMLOT MUST BE 100% FREE FOR ALL USERS.**

No paid subscriptions, premium memberships, credits, paywalls or locked features.

Do not add a pricing page.

Individual third-party opportunities may have entry fees, but Simlot must clearly display these fees and provide filters for free-entry opportunities.

### 5. GLOBAL OPPORTUNITY SEARCH ENGINE

Build a searchable, structured database designed to support at least 500 verified opportunities initially and thousands as the platform grows.

Each opportunity must include:

- Unique opportunity ID
- Opportunity title
- Organising institution
- Official website
- Direct application link
- Description
- Opportunity category
- Subject areas
- Minimum and maximum eligible ages
- Eligible education stages
- Eligible countries
- Residency and nationality requirements, where applicable
- Online, in-person or hybrid format
- Location
- Application opening date
- Application deadline
- Event or programme dates
- Entry fee and currency
- Whether financial assistance is available
- Scholarship or prize value, if applicable
- Required qualifications or experience
- Individual or team participation
- Expected time commitment
- Official eligibility requirements
- Source URL
- Last verified date
- Verification status

**Critical data integrity requirements:**

Do not invent opportunities, organisers, URLs, deadlines, eligibility criteria or award amounts.

Only publish opportunities supported by verifiable official sources.

If fewer than 500 verified opportunities are available initially, publish the verified records and show the true count. Do not fabricate entries to reach 500.

Support bulk CSV and JSON imports, duplicate detection and administrator review.

Show "Deadline not confirmed" when an official deadline cannot be verified.

Mark outdated records for review and prevent expired opportunities from appearing as currently open.

### 7. PERSONALISATION AND MATCHING ENGINE

Create a recommendation system that ranks opportunities based on:

1. Confirmed eligibility
2. Subject interest match
3. Education stage
4. Age requirements
5. Geographic eligibility
6. User ambitions
7. Budget
8. Deadline availability
9. Time commitment

Use hard eligibility filters before calculating relevance scores.

Never describe an opportunity as definitely eligible when its requirements have not been verified.

For every recommendation, explain:

**Why this matches you**

Example:

"This competition matches your interest in mathematics, accepts participants in your age group and allows international online entries."

Show a match score only when based on a documented, consistent algorithm.

Provide a clear distinction between:
- Eligible
- Potentially eligible — check requirements
- Not eligible

Never claim that participation guarantees admission to a university or improves acceptance chances by a specific percentage.
